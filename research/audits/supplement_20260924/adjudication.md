## Key proof checks and trace reconciliation

- **A.1:** The radial integral differentiates in $r^2$ to $1/(2\sqrt{r^2+\varepsilon^2})$. The constant term cancels against the signed measure $F-G$. For each $t>0$, the Gaussian-kernel energy is $(4\pi t)^{-K/2}\int e^{-\|\omega\|^2/(4t)}|\varphi_F-\varphi_G|^2d\omega$, nonnegative and zero only for equal laws. Finite first moments make distance expectations finite; the positive mixture weight gives strictness for every fixed $\varepsilon\ge0$.
- **A.2:** On the finite-risk domain, $R(\widehat F)-R(P)\le b_n+2U_n+o_n$. The infimum comparison uses $\widehat R(F)\le R(F)+U_n$; no minimizer is needed. The risk identity and $e_a\ge c$ give factor $2/c$. Compactness, continuity, strict propriety, and the diameter bound justify integrated $W_1$ convergence, including the separately handled empty separated-pair set.
- **A.2a:** The treatment-tilted law in each cell is still a mixture of $P_a(x')$ inside that cell, so its $W_1$ error is at most $L_X\sqrt d\,\ell_n$. Particle sampling gives $E D_\varepsilon(P_M,G)=M^{-1}E d_\varepsilon(Z,Z')$, hence risk approximation $O(M^{-1})$. A net over $2M_nJ_n$ particle locations has log-size $O(KM_nJ_n\log n)$; balancing cell error, particle error, and concentration yields exponent $1/(d+3)$. This remains a comparison-class rate conditional on approximate global minimization, not a booster guarantee.
- **A.4:** Bounded inverse propensities and the conditional residual-variance bound imply conditional score-difference second moment $O_p(r_m^2+r_e^2)=o_p(1)$. Its conditional mean is the product remainder $o_p(n^{-1/2})$. Foldwise conditional Chebyshev, finite fold count, and the iid CLT yield the expansion despite overlapping training sets. $EH^2<\infty$ suffices for the true-score LLN and variance estimate. The stronger uniform $q>2$ conditions of the cited DML theorem are not imported.
- **A.5:** The normalized denominator tends to $f_X(x)$. The local residual variance is $f_X(x)v_h(x)\int L^2$; the Lyapunov ratio is $O((Nb^d)^{-\delta/2})$. Undersmoothing removes Holder bias. For variance consistency, truncate squared residuals using the local $2+\delta$ moment bound, apply the bounded kernel LLN, then remove truncation; conditional Markov and weighted Cauchy–Schwarz handle fitted-score errors. Fourth moments are unnecessary under the stated local conditions.
- **A.6:** Couple common mixture mass through component laws and move only unmatched mass to or from zero. This yields $L_C|\widehat\pi-\pi|+\pi W_1(\widehat P^+,P^+)$. Adding and subtracting $\pi\widehat\mu^+$ gives the scalar bound with $2B_h|\widehat\pi-\pi|$ and the same asymmetric component factor. The endpoints, including $\pi=0$ with the stated component convention, are covered.

The trace's A.2 infimum-comparison direction and A.5 raw-numerator variance sentence were inaccurate as isolated statements. The corrected proof steps above resolve both; neither invalidates the theorem.
